The-Olive-Tree-Soap-Company-Full-Stack
I will be creating a simple website for my family soap business called The Olive Tree Soap Co. The business specializes in creating home made all natural skin care products such as soap, lip balm, deodorant, and moisturising cream. On our site you will be able to browse the catalog and use filters and a search bar to find the product you are looking for.

Admin interface: Add/Edit products, view orders, respond to customer reviews. Front-End User Interface: Search products, view product details, rate product, add to shopping cart, checkout, and view order history.
