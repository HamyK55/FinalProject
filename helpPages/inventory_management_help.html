Inventory Management Help

This page lets you review and update the products already in the store.

What you can do here

  • Update product names, descriptions, prices, stock, category, and active status.
  • Upload a new image for a product.
  • Check whether products are currently visible to customers.

Updating a product

  • Click the action button for the product you want to change.
  • Change the fields you need, then save the update.
  • Make sure prices are entered as numbers and stock is zero or above.

Image updates

If you upload a new image for a product, it will be added as a new image and the first uploaded image becomes the primary image.

Tips

  • Keep product names clear and consistent.
  • Use the active flag carefully so customers can only see products that are ready.
  • Check stock often to avoid overselling.
