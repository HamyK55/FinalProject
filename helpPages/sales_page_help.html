Sales Page Help

This page gives a simple sales summary for the store.

What the page shows

  • Total units sold: how many items were sold through fulfilled orders.
  • Total revenue: revenue from fulfilled orders.
  • Pending units and revenue: items and money tied to orders that are not yet fulfilled.

How to read the report

  • Use the Overview section to see the overall picture.
  • Use the product breakdowns to see which products are performing well.
  • Use the pending section to spot orders that still need attention.

Tips

  • Check this page regularly to track store performance.
  • Review which products are selling the most.
  • Watch pending revenue if you want to understand orders that still need handling.
